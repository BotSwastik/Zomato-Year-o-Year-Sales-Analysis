## 🔎 Zomato YoY Sales Analysis

### 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato’s sales data to uncover year-over-year growth trends, customer behavior, and category performance, providing actionable insights for business expansion and marketing strategies.

---

### 🔎 Key Insights

-  📈 Revenue Growth: Sales showed a steady ~18% YoY growth, with festive seasons (Oct–Dec) consistently driving the highest spikes.

-  🍽️ Category Trends: Fast food & desserts dominated growth, contributing nearly 40% of incremental sales YoY.

-  📍 Regional Performance: Tier-1 cities led sales volume, but Tier-2 cities showed faster growth (25% YoY), signaling expansion potential.

-  ⏱️ Customer Behavior: Peak ordering times clustered around 7–10 PM, with weekends driving 20% higher order volume than weekdays.

-  💳 Order Value: Average order value grew by ~12% YoY, suggesting higher customer willingness to spend per order.
